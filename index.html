All built for Jesus Christ Saves Global Outreach (JCSGO), a church running services, rentals, and events across five buildings.

Featured · Booking platform

JCSGO Central Request Portal

A Next.js 16 app for reserving any of JCSGO's 40 rooms across 5 buildings, no login required to send a request. Every request gets checked against a Postgres constraint that makes double-booking impossible, not just discouraged.

Approvals, rejections, and edits text the requester automatically, sent through AkeriusSMS, a small Kotlin Android app that turns a spare phone into the church's SMS gateway.
